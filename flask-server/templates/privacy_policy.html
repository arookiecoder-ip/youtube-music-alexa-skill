Privacy

Music Box stores account details, password hashes, sign-in sessions, preferences, device information, and playback state to provide this service.

Connecting Amazon or YouTube Music can store provider-session information on this server. Requests to those services are also handled by the provider.

You can review your sessions, sign out other devices, disconnect connected services, and delete your account from the account page.

Operational and security logs record requests and account activity. Contact the operator of this Music Box server for deployment-specific logging, backup, retention, and privacy questions.
